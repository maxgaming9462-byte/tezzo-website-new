Delete your Tezzo account

From inside the app

Open Tezzo and go to Profile → Account → Delete account. You will be asked to confirm. The deletion happens straight away.

If you no longer have the app

Email Tezzocar@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, and ask for your account to be deleted. Writing from your registered address is how we confirm the request is really yours. We reply within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your name, email address and phone number
  • Your profile photo, bio, date of birth, gender and city
  • Your emergency contact
  • Your postal address
  • Your saved payment and payout details, including any bank or UPI information
  • Your vehicles
  • Your ID verification documents
  • Your notifications and search history
  • Your referral code and referral link

Any ride or booking of yours that is still upcoming is cancelled, and the account can no longer be signed in to.

What is kept, and why

  • Rides and bookings you already completed, and the payment records attached to them. These are a financial record and also belong to the other person on that trip, so they are kept — but they no longer carry your name or contact details.
  • An empty, anonymised profile row, so those past trips do not break. It holds no personal information about you.

This cannot be undone

Deleting your account is permanent. You can sign up again later, but nothing from the old account comes back.
