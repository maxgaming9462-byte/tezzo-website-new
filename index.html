Tezzo

Share the cost of the trip, not just the road.

Tezzo connects drivers who have empty seats with passengers heading the same way. Intercity travel across India, split between the people already making the journey.

Coming to Google Play — Android package com.tezzo.app.

What you can do

  • Find a ride

    Search intercity routes by city and date, filter and sort the results, and see the full price before you book.

  • Offer your seats

    Publish a ride with your route, stops, timing, price and seat count. Approve each request yourself, or let bookings auto-accept.

  • Talk before you go

    Message the driver or passenger in the app. Phone numbers stay hidden until a booking is confirmed.

  • Know who you travel with

    Profiles carry ratings and reviews from real completed rides. Government ID verification is available for drivers.

  • Women-only rides

    Women drivers can mark a ride as women-only. The restriction is enforced on the server when a seat is booked.

  • Help when you need it

    An SOS button shares your live location and ride details with your emergency contact, and alerts our team.

  • Follow the journey

    Passengers can watch the driver approach in real time once a ride starts.

  • Pay directly, no card

    Fares are settled between you and the driver over UPI or cash. Tezzo never handles or stores card details.
