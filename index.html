How it works

Two sides of the same trip. One of you is already driving it.

If you need a seat

  1. 1

    Search your route

    Enter where you are leaving from, where you are going and the date. Filter and sort what comes back — price, time, rating.

  2. 2

    Request your seats

    You see the seat price and any optional add-ons before you commit. If the driver has auto-accept on, the booking confirms instantly; otherwise they review your request.

  3. 3

    Chat, then travel

    Message the driver in the app. Phone numbers are exchanged only once the booking is confirmed, and you can watch the car approach once the ride starts.

  4. 4

    Pay by UPI and rate

    Pay the driver for the seat by UPI. There is no Tezzo fee added on top. Afterwards you rate each other.

If you have a car

  1. 1

    Publish the ride

    A guided flow takes you through route and stops, date and time, seats, price per seat, optional add-ons and your ride preferences.

  2. 2

    Decide how requests come in

    Auto-accept confirms bookings instantly. Leave it off and every request waits for you to approve it.

  3. 3

    Drive from the dashboard

    Your dashboard holds the day’s requests and passengers. You start the ride when you are actually at the pickup point, and your location is then shared with the people on board.

  4. 4

    Get paid out

    Add a payout method under Profile → Account. Bank payouts land in 2–3 business days; UPI payouts are instant. Your full history sits in Earnings & Payouts.

Driving anyway? Fill the empty seats.

You are making the journey either way. Publishing it takes a few minutes, and the seats you were not using pay for part of the fuel.

  • You set the price

    You choose the price per seat when you publish. Tezzo adds no platform fee on top of it, so what the passenger pays for the seat is what you set.

  • Optional paid add-ons

    Offer extras such as extra luggage space, each with its own price and quantity limit. Passengers pay only for what they pick.

  • Up to 5 vehicles

    Keep up to five vehicles on your account and choose one per ride. Premium Driver status raises the limit to ten.

  • Preferences that stick

    Travel Preferences save your defaults for chattiness, music, pets, smoking and luggage, and pre-fill every ride you publish.

  • Women-only rides

    Women drivers can restrict a ride to women passengers. It is enforced in the database when the seat is booked, not just hidden in the app.

  • No-show claims

    If a passenger does not turn up, raise a no-show claim from the app and our team reviews it.

Payouts go to a bank account or a UPI ID you add under Profile → Account. Bank transfers take 2–3 business days; UPI is instant.

Trust & safety

You are getting into a car with someone you have not met. These are the things the app does about that.

  • Government ID verification

    Submit an Aadhaar, Driving License, PAN or Passport. Tezzo reviews it, and an approved profile carries a checkmark badge.

  • A trust page per person

    Tap the checkmark on any profile to see verification status, how long they have been a member, rides completed and rating.

  • Your number stays private

    Phone numbers are never on a public profile. They are revealed only to the other side of a confirmed booking, and to nobody else.

  • Ratings from real rides

    Ratings and reviews can only be left after a ride you actually took, so the score on a profile is built from completed trips.

  • SOS while you travel

    From an active ride you can call 112, call your emergency contact, or share your live trip and location. Every press is logged for our team.

  • Report anything off

    Report a driver, a passenger, a ride or a chat from inside the app. Our trust team reviews every report.

  • Account security

    Two-factor authentication and biometric unlock are both available, and your session is held in the device’s own secure keystore.

  • No card details anywhere

    Tezzo does not take card payments and stores no card numbers, CVVs or full bank account numbers.

Always pay through UPI as shown in the app — never send or accept money outside it for a Tezzo ride, and check that the driver, vehicle and route match your booking before you get in.

Questions

The same answers the Help & Support screen gives inside the app.

Booking & Rides

How do I book a ride?

Search for a route on the Find a ride tab, pick a matching ride, and confirm your seats. You can book for yourself and any saved passengers travelling with you.

Can I book more than one ride on the same route and date?

No — you can only hold one active booking per route (from city, to city, date) at a time. If you need to change rides, cancel your current booking first.

What does "auto-accept" mean for a ride?

When a driver enables auto-accept, bookings are confirmed instantly. If it's off, the driver reviews each request before confirming.

What is "women only" on a ride?

Some drivers mark a ride as reserved for women passengers only. If you try to book one and this doesn't apply to you, you'll see "This ride is reserved for women passengers only."

Can I set a seat position or chattiness preference?

Yes — your Travel Preferences (in Profile > Account) let you set defaults for chattiness, music, pets, smoking, and luggage space, which pre-fill new rides you publish. You can still change any of them per ride.

Payments (UPI)

What payment methods does Tezzo support?

Tezzo currently supports UPI only. Card payments are not yet available.

How do I get paid as a driver?

Set up your payout method (bank account or UPI ID) under Profile > Account > Payout Bank / UPI. Bank payouts are transferred within 2-3 business days; UPI payouts are instant.

Where can I see my earnings history?

Profile > Account > Earnings & Payouts shows your full payout history.

Is my payment information secure?

Only your UPI ID is stored — Tezzo never stores card numbers, CVVs, or full bank account details.

How is the total price of my ride calculated?

The total is your seat price (price per seat × number of seats) plus any optional add-ons you choose during booking, like extra luggage space. There is no separate Tezzo platform fee added on top.

What are add-ons and are they optional?

Add-ons are extras a driver may offer when publishing a ride (e.g. extra luggage space), each with its own price and quantity limit. They're entirely optional — only what you select is added to your total.

Cancellations & Refunds

Can I cancel a booked ride?

Yes, both drivers and passengers can cancel before the ride starts, from the Bookings tab.

Does cancelling affect my account?

Late cancellations (close to the scheduled pickup time) may affect your account rating and, for drivers, future visibility in ride search results.

How long do refunds take?

Refunds for cancelled or disputed rides are handled per our cancellation policy and may take several business days to reflect, depending on your payment provider.

Where can I see my refund status?

Profile > Account > Payments & refunds lists your refund history and status.

Safety & Verification

What does "ID Verified" mean?

A verified user has submitted a government ID (Aadhaar, Driving License, PAN, or Passport) that Tezzo has reviewed and approved. Look for the checkmark badge on a profile.

How do I verify my own ID?

Go to Profile > Verify your Govt. ID, choose your document type, and upload clear front/back photos. Review usually happens shortly after submission.

Where can I see someone's full trust info before riding with them?

Tap the verified checkmark on their profile to open the Trust & Safety screen — it shows verification status, member-since duration, rides completed, and rating.

When is a driver or passenger's phone number shared?

Phone numbers are only revealed to each other once a booking is confirmed — never before, and never to anyone outside that specific booking.

How do I report a safety concern?

Use "Report a problem" from your Profile, or the report option on a specific driver/passenger/ride. See "Still need help?" below for a shortcut.

How do I stay safe from scams while using Tezzo?

Always pay and receive payment through UPI as shown in the app — never send or accept money outside the app for a Tezzo ride. Confirm the driver, vehicle, and route match what's shown in your booking before getting in, and meet at the agreed pickup point in a public area rather than a private location.

What if someone asks me to pay outside the app or change plans last-minute?

Treat this as a warning sign — legitimate rides on Tezzo are paid for as shown in the app. Don't send money through any other method, and use "Report a problem" to let us know right away.

What should I do if something feels off during a ride?

Trust your instincts. Don't get in if the vehicle or driver doesn't match your booking details, meet in a public place for pickup, and use the Report option on the profile, chat, or booking to flag anything suspicious — our trust team reviews every report.

Driver-specific questions

How many vehicles can I add?

Up to 5 vehicles. Premium Driver status raises this to 10.

What is Premium Driver status?

A ribbon badge shown on your profile and ride cards, plus a higher vehicle limit. It's manually tracked for now — contact support if you believe you should have it.

How do add-ons work when publishing a ride?

You can offer optional paid add-ons (e.g. extra luggage space) when posting a ride. Passengers choose and pay for any add-ons during booking.

Can I set default ride preferences so I don't re-enter them every time?

Yes — Travel Preferences (Profile > Account) saves your defaults for chattiness, pets, smoking, and luggage space, and pre-fills them whenever you publish a new ride.

Account & Privacy

Who can see my profile information?

Your name, photo, rating, ride count, and verification/premium badges are visible to anyone. Your bio and city are private by default — you can choose to share them in Edit personal details.

Is my phone number ever public?

No. Your phone number is never shown on your public profile — it's only shared with a confirmed booking counterpart.

How do I change my email or password?

Profile > Account has separate options for Change Email and Password, plus Two-Factor Authentication.

What happens if I delete my account?

Your personal info is erased immediately. Completed ride records are retained for legal compliance, and the account can't be used to sign back in.
